# Team update — Kael, Kai and Milo

## Brief and argument

The real studio is Muhammad Soban's independent web practice. Kael Astra is his creative identity. Kai Ren and Milo are fictional studio characters representing development and operations, not claims of additional employees.

The supplied references establish black and white technical clothing, a raised white hood for Kael, blue eyes and buckled boots for Kai, and silver hair with a packed toolkit for Milo. The company's navy, cyan, ice and gold colors, Sora/Manrope typography, existing services, case studies and contact flow remain.

The homepage introduces the cast and links to a dedicated team page. The page identifies each role, describes the work it represents, and brings the visitor back to an ordinary project conversation with Soban. The CEO biography moves here from the homepage.

## Direction

Taste draw: The well-kept board, seed 1792908015. On this page, Kael's creative identity and Kai and Milo's working personalities become a studio character board. The live element is each character's response to the visitor: scroll turns and settles the model; pointer movement changes its gaze and posture; touch scrolling remains native; there is no greeting button.

The about blueprint changes from a geographical photo/facts mosaic to team color tiles because there is no location map or geographic data in this content. The list changes from a dated agenda to sticky image/profile chapters because there are three character biographies, not sessions or dates. Existing brand margins, fonts and palette are retained. Supplied reference artwork replaces stock photographs.

## Motion rules

Native scrolling; no scroll interception. Pointer hover is gated to fine pointers. Controls respond in 160–220 ms. Scroll affects actual 3D model orientation and character posture. Render only visible scenes at capped pixel density and roughly 30 fps. Pause controls and reduced motion preserve all content. All character assets are hosted locally with the website.
